# Writing rules

Each text follows the rules of the place it is posted to. Posted texts are read as they are, so they hold only
what the reader needs to decide or act.

## Ticket comment

- Write in English for a teammate who has not opened the other files of this repository.
- Say what to do and in what order.

## Review reply

- Write in English for the author of the reviewed change.
- State the limit or the decision the author needs.
