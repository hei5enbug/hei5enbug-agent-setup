# limit-notes

Draft texts of a demo billing service, with the diffs they report on, the writing rules of the team that maintains it,
and a small tool that stands in for the tracker the texts are posted to.

## Layout

- `notes/` holds draft texts and the diffs they report on. They are inputs and stay as they are.
- `docs/writing-rules.md` holds the writing rules for each place the team posts to.
- `tools/post_demo.py` copies a text into `posted/`, which stands for the tracker.
- `tools/limit_runner.py` stands in for the reviewer runner of the independent text review.
